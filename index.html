Schoolbucks

Schoolbucks is a rewards platform currently in development, focused on users in South Africa and Zimbabwe. The platform will allow members to complete offers from partner networks and earn rewards for qualifying activity, with the goal of offering flexible payout options such as mobile money and bank transfer upon launch. The system is being built with an emphasis on user verification, reward transparency, and account security.

  • Offer-Based Rewards
  • Wallet & Balance Tracking
  • Mobile Money & Bank Payouts
